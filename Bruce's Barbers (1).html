About us

I opened the shop in 2019 after years of cutting hair in other people's shops around District 1 and Phú Nhuận. Bình Thạnh didn't have many places doing proper fades and razor work at a fair price, so we started one.

There are four of us now. Most of our customers are regulars from the neighbourhood, students from the universities nearby and people who work around Văn Thánh. Booking gets you the barber you want. Walk-ins are fine too, there's tea while you wait.

— Bruce Le, owner

Good to know

  • Students: 10% off Tuesday to Thursday with a student card.
  • Bring a photo of the cut you want. It helps a lot.
  • Payment: cash, bank transfer (QR) or card.
  • Parking: free motorbike parking out front.
  • Closed Mondays. Last walk-in 30 minutes before closing.
